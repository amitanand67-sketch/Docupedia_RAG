Lumora IT Helpdesk FAQ

Last updated 8 January 2027. Raise anything not covered here at helpdesk.lumora.example or call extension 4357.

How do I connect to the VPN?

Install GlobalProtect from the Self Service app, enter the portal vpn.lumora.example and sign in with your Lumora account and authenticator app. The VPN is required for PeopleHub and the internal wiki when you are outside the office.

How do I reset my password?

Go to password.lumora.example, verify with your authenticator app and choose a new password of at least 14 characters. Passwords expire every 180 days. After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account locks for 15 minutes.

What laptop will I get?

Engineers receive a 16-inch laptop with 32 GB RAM; everyone else receives a 14-inch laptop with 16 GB RAM. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years.

Can I install my own software?

Approved tools are available in the Self Service app. For anything else, open a Software Request ticket; the security team reviews requests within 3 working days.

Who do I contact about a lost device?

Report a lost or stolen device to security@lumora.example within 1 hour so it can be remotely wiped.
